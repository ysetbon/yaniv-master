# counter2: every version measured

Dev test unless a row says otherwise: `python -m training.yaniv.heuristic2_eval counter2 --against OPP`
(6,000 mirrored rounds seed 91000; matches seed 91001, 1,000 or 1,500 as stated). Margin is the opponent's points
minus counter2's, a round; ± is the 95% range. "Played out" rows come from `heuristic2_study differ`: wherever two
players would move differently the round is finished both ways on the same hidden cards (seeds 930000+).

Baselines on the dev seeds (`dev-baselines.txt`), against the old counter: trained net +1.56 ±0.33, 64.5% of 1,500
matches; `yaniv-net-5cards-handmade` +1.77 ±0.34, 72.2%; `yaniv-net-handmade` +1.75 ±0.34, 71.5%.

| v1 | First whole player. Opponent total modelled exactly up to 12 points: hidden cards lean low (HIDDEN_BETA), and the cards it kept are capped by the single it last threw. Call at a modelled catch risk of 0.12 or less.
